Who we are

Welcome to Tender Care

We have been educating children for over forty years. Our goal is to create learning environments that engage each child.

About Us

Embracing innovation

We believe that when young minds are nurtured and curiosity is sparked, it motivates children to ask questions, seek answers, and discover new possibilities.

Pursuing Excellence

Our schools embody the NAEYC accreditation standards which correlates with children's greater readiness and success in school and beyond; and overall healthier lifestyles.

Growing by Learning

Learn to celebrate diversity in a spirit of understanding and tolerance and develop a positive regard and awareness of other people.

Global Citizenship

Children become global citizens when they learn about the world beyond their immediate surroundings, understand the interconnectedness of societies, and begin to care about global issues.
