Jill Blair

  • Location: Whitehall
  • Email: whi@tendercarelc.org

I graduated from Robert Morris University with my bachelor’s degree in Communications and Journalism. Quickly after graduating, I realized I wanted to pursue education so I returned to Robert Morris to get certified in Elementary Education. I began working for Tender Care Whitehall in 2008 as a preschool teacher. At first, I thought the job I accepted as a preschool teacher would be a temporary stepping stone until I could get a job teaching in a school district. Within a few short months of accepting a preschool teaching position, I accepted an Assistant Director position and knew my path would be different than what I originally envisioned. A few years later I was promoted to Director at our Whitehall school. I can’t imagine my career path being different and I’m thankful for the opportunity to work with so many amazing coworkers and families. In my free time I love to spend time with my husband and 3 boys. We love to travel, laugh, act goofy and make lots of fun memories along the way!
