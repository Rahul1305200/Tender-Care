New Castle

At Tender Care Learning Center of New Castle, Pennsylvania, we strive to make each and every child feel welcome and cared for every day, as we care for their physical, emotional, social and academic development. Our early education professionals delight in helping children explore the world around them and develop a lifelong love of learning through play. We hope you’ll visit us today to experience the Tender Care difference for yourself!
