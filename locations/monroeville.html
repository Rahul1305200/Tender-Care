Monroeville

At Tender Care Learning Center of Monroeville, Pennsylvania, we’d like to welcome you to our warm, friendly and knowledgeable school. Our teachers and staff are nurturing and outgoing, and we pride ourselves on knowing each child’s unique talents, skills and learning styles. Our award-winning curriculum inspires a lifelong love of learning and provides a solid foundation for children to succeed emotionally, mentally and physically in life.
